Next steps

Launch and the first 90 days

1
Launch
We key the finalized settings, send the test lead, and enable the campaign on the confirmed launch date.
2
Delivery check
Over the first three days we confirm impressions, clicks, leads and calls are flowing, that web leads are landing in Ricochet360, and that the lead line rings through.
3
Dispositions
Mark each lead as contacted, quoted or bound as it is worked. Before each review we send a short export guide; the export is imported into GOAL so the review reports on contact rate, quote rate and cost per policy.
4
Review cadence
Reviews run every two weeks. The first 90 days are judged on contact rate and quote rate; cost per policy is reported once policies have had time to bind.
